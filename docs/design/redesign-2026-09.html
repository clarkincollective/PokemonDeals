Method

How a listing gets here

Three checks, in order. A listing that fails any of them is withheld and counted on the integrity report.

Full methodology →
01 · SCAN

Every eBay marketplace, every 15 minutes (US)

New listings are discovered continuously across six eBay sites and re-checked on a rolling schedule. A listing that ends or sells stops being shown; each card prints when it was last checked.

02 · MATCH

One exact printing, or nothing

Name, set, collector number, card form and language all have to agree with the catalogue. Jumbo, World Championship, prerelease and altered cards are withheld — 34 active listings are, today.

03 · COMPARE

Against recent solds for that condition

The reference is recent sold data for the same printing and condition, labelled and dated. Raw is never compared with graded. If no trustworthy reference exists, no saving is claimed.

Transparency

Straight answers

Are the cards listed here authentic?

No third-party site can guarantee an eBay listing is authentic. We withhold listings that fail an image-based screen, that use replica, proxy or altered-card wording, or whose printing doesn't match the catalogue — and we never label a listing "verified authentic". The photos, the seller's history and eBay's Money Back Guarantee remain your checks.

Where does the market reference come from?

Recent sold data for the same printing and condition, recorded by date and shown beside every listing. It's a reference, not a guaranteed sale price.

Do you sell cards or take a cut?

No. We hold no stock and take no paid placement. Links to eBay are affiliate links (eBay Partner Network); you pay eBay's listed price. Independent, one operator, no store.

Why is a listing sometimes missing shipping?

When eBay records no shipping charge we say "not confirmed" and measure nothing from it. A saving is only claimed against a delivered total we actually hold.

How current is a deal?

Each card shows when it was first found and when it was last checked. A page for a listing that has ended says so and points to the card's current listings.
