## 3. Product, business and design mandate

### What success means

The commercial loop is qualified discovery, understandable comparison, correct outbound eBay action and a possible attributed purchase. Recognised affiliate earnings after reversals, with currency and period stated, are the revenue evidence. A click is not a sale. Catalogue and educational visitors should still accomplish their own tasks even when no suitable offer exists.

The strategy is international marketplace support with a documented US-first organic emphasis. James operates from Australia; that does not turn every price, page or audience into an Australian one. Preserve the red/white magnifying-glass identity and avoid implying official Pokemon/Nintendo affiliation.

### Premium experience and human behaviour

Make offer identity, condition, price meaning and destination understandable at a glance. Use attractive real imagery, consistent typography, compact cards, restrained accents, predictable controls and legible secondary information. Keep the eBay action obvious and details/save subordinate. Avoid enormous mobile artwork, dense microcopy, overlapping badges and duplicate inventories competing for attention.

Use recognition, manageable choices, progressive disclosure and visible feedback. Support both exploratory bargain hunting and precise card identification. Explain comparison limitations where the decision occurs. Preserve useful filter/back-navigation context. Respect reduced motion, keyboard focus and touch targets.

Do not use false scarcity, unsupported popularity, fake savings, automatic urgency, collection-completion pressure or obstructive dialogs. Aesthetic preference, task success and commercial effect are separate outcomes. The existing overhaul document treats its psychology applications as hypotheses, not measured conversion wins.

Evaluate novice and experienced collectors with neutral tasks: identify a printing, explain shipping, distinguish a current bid from a buy-it-now price, find a suitable offer, save and return, or mark missing checklist entries. Fixture-based comprehension checks can proceed without paid calls or purchases. New participant outreach requires its own authorisation.

## 5. Offer truth, search and image integrity

### The comparison contract

Identify the exact printing before pricing: product ID, name, set, collector number, language, edition/variant, raw condition or grader/grade and offer type. Resolve ambiguity rather than treating a collector number as globally unique. Price must not decide which identity wins.

PokemonPriceTracker is the documented market-reference provider. Provider-derived references and repeated snapshots are not the site's own independent eBay sold transactions. Keep reference time, listing discovery time and exact verification time distinct. A checked listing is not authenticated merchandise.

The reported integrity pipeline includes condition parsing, suspicious-discount deeper verification, variant/language/lot guards, seller checks, graded confidence, stale/sold handling, auction rules, image checks, allocation and variety. Historical parameters include a 10% deal threshold, a 25% sanity floor, seller feedback of at least 95% with at least 10 entries, and checks against large reference divergence. These are implementation clues to verify, not fresh permission to retune thresholds.

Raw condition must never silently default to Near Mint. Weaker conditions require supported comparable tiers; grading companies and grades must match. A current auction bid is not the final purchase price or a guaranteed saving. Preserve existing contested-auction qualification rules. Native currency, reference conversion and shipping must be intelligible.

**Shipping is a known data limitation and a current review concern.** A stored zero historically represents both free and unstated shipping. “No shipping charge listed” is different from “free shipping.” A labelled listing total must include the actual recorded components; a component fixture cannot prove the cache projection preserved those components.

### Prefixed collector-number search: already fixed

Production fix `c6d670c` recognises matching-prefix slash forms before the truncating hash/prefixed patterns, generates stored-case variants and gives whole-number matches ranking credit. The shipped Supabase equality lookup is case-sensitive; the in-memory lookup lowercases rows. This adapter difference is why parser-only tests were inadequate.

| Example | Reported resolver/database evidence |
|---|---|
| `RC3/RC32`, `charmander rc3/rc32` | Exact Charmander, product `113744` |
| RC siblings | `RC5/RC32` = Charizard `113746`; `RC17/RC32` = Flabebe `113759`. Never match just `/RC32`. |
| `sv49/sv94` | Charizard GX `197780`; no longer truncates to `sv49` plus junk name text |
| `h5/h32`, `gg2/gg70` | Padded stored forms resolve; H05/H32 also preserves multiple-set ambiguity |
| `sv94/sv122` | Cinccino `232480`, stored `SV094/SV122`; this demonstrates numerator padding, not denominator padding |
| Numeric controls | Charizard 4/102 and 004/102 remain equivalent; Umbreon 215/203 remains correct; multi-set ambiguity remains |
| Wrong subject | `pikachu rc3/rc32` remains `subject_collector_mismatch`, exact null |

The survey found 532 same-prefix slash rows across SV/TG/GG/H/RC/S, including 162 zero-padded rows. Four S-family Box Toppers remain excluded by the pre-existing name guard. Both-half padding is defensive coverage; denominator padding was not demonstrated. Variant lists were reported up to 12 terms. These dated observations are not today's catalogue totals.

Evidence establishes the resolver's decision changing from provider fallback to exact-card, not measured paid-call savings. Live production search was deliberately not requested because fallback reaches the paid PPT cards API.
